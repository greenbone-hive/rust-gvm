# 2026-09-07 — Typed Execution Migration Completes and Facade Split Is Chosen

## Typed Execution Track
- Pull requests [#577](https://github.com/greenbone-hive/rust-gvm/pull/577), [#579](https://github.com/greenbone-hive/rust-gvm/pull/579), [#580](https://github.com/greenbone-hive/rust-gvm/pull/580), and [#581](https://github.com/greenbone-hive/rust-gvm/pull/581) merged the remaining report-format, TLS, report-mutation, discovery, and system-administration typed execution families into `next`, completing the planned family migration tracked by [#523](https://github.com/greenbone-hive/rust-gvm/issues/523).
